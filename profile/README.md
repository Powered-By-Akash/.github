### 🏛️ About Powered By Akash (PBA)

**Powered By Akash (PBA)** is an engineering and digital product ecosystem founded in February 2021 by **Akash Tanna**. We specialize in developing robust computational engines, automating civil and structural calculations, and building high-speed digital web platforms.

Our repositories distribute verified algorithms designed to eliminate manual spreadsheet errors, automate repetitive AutoCAD drafting, and solve complex hydraulic and structural systems.

